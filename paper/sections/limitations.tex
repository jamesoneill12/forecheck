\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Synthetic data.} Every number outside \S\ref{sec:external} is on data
from forecheck's own generator and measures whether an arm learned the
generator's label function. No human-labelled or production-traffic
evaluation exists and no real-world safety claim is made. The blind judge
checks whether a label is observable in the text, not whether it is right; it
finds \texttt{insufficient\_context} unrecoverable, and every number on that
dimension carries that caveat. The judge is one frontier model with one
prompt.

\textbf{Generator regularities.} The model may exploit template regularities
in how the generator writes clauses and entitlement lists. The phrasing and
kind splits only partly rule this out: phrasing generalizes, kind does not,
which is consistent with a lookup over kinds. The \texttt{privilege\_escalation}
fields were not rendered before v4, so every v2/v3 number on that dimension
(0.46--0.53 AUPRC) reflects a data defect. The v1--v5 injection leak means
every synthetic injection number before v6, and the judge's $\kappa{=}0.905$
on it, is on text where a key-presence detector would score 1.0; the trained
arms scored about 0.80, so the leak did not inflate the score but determined
what was learned, and only the external pairs could reveal it. Retrained on
v6, every other synthetic dimension is within 0.01 of v4 except
\texttt{policy\_conflict} on \texttt{heldout\_policy\_kind} (0.692 at 2B,
inside the seed band; 0.767 at 8B, 0.08 below the v4 mean).

\textbf{Seeds.} Three 2B seeds on v4 and on v5 agree on macro AUPRC to within
0.001, but the \texttt{heldout\_policy\_kind} \texttt{policy\_conflict} cell
spans 0.06 (sd 0.03), so smaller differences on that cell are noise. 8B has
two seeds on v4 (test macro 0.956 and 0.948, the gap entirely on
\texttt{insufficient\_context}); both v6 checkers are single-seed, so
2B-vs-8B comparisons on v6 are unqualified.

\textbf{External labels are partly self-written and uneven.} AgentDojo's
policy predicates and InjecAgent's five policies are ours; injection and scope
labels are the benchmarks' own. Banking has four tool-specific policies, the
other suites one generic policy each, so policy transfer rests on banking and
InjecAgent. AgentDojo's \texttt{unauthorized\_scope} counts harmless auxiliary
reads as out of scope. The InjecAgent port fills principal and scope with
placeholders, which the 8B v6 head reads as authorising the attacker call.
The v6 checkers have not been run on the four-suite export. Calibration does
not transfer without a few hundred in-domain labels.

\textbf{Other.} A single English template generator produces every synthetic
example. The cost table in \S\ref{sec:composition} is the default severity
tier. Generator seed nondeterminism was found and fixed mid-project; v2/v3
data are not byte-reproducible from their configs.
