\section{Are the labels observable? A blind judge}
\label{sec:labelvalidity}

Is each label recoverable from the rendered text by a reader who never sees
the generator's latent scenario? A frontier model (gpt-5.6-sol, one prompt,
low reasoning effort), given only the rendered context and the eleven
dimension definitions, judged 600 v4 rows stratified to at least 20 positives
and 20 negatives per dimension, once with the full rendering and once
identity-stripped. The definitions are the generator's own docstrings, so
this tests observability under a shared definition, not definitional
independence. Agreement with the generator is not ground truth, but low
agreement is informative: the label then depends on information the
generator tracked and never rendered, and a model scoring well on it has
learned a generator regularity. $\kappa$ is on 600 rows without intervals.

\textbf{Three tiers.} \emph{Recoverable} ($\kappa > 0.7$):
\texttt{privilege\_escalation}, \texttt{policy\_conflict} (judge recall 0.89;
disagreements are the judge reading a clause more strictly than the
predicate), and, on leaked text, injection. \emph{Definitional disagreement}
($0.2 \le \kappa \le 0.5$): six dimensions read two coherent ways; the judge
flags twice as many \texttt{unauthorized\_scope} rows because it counts a
mismatch with the agent's stated objective as out of scope, while the
generator's label is entitlement-based. \emph{Not recoverable} ($\kappa <
0.1$): \texttt{insufficient\_context} (the judge calls the context sufficient
on 50 of 58 generator-positive rows) and \texttt{sensitive\_data\_exposure},
whose label requires the destination to leave the tenant, a fact the
rendering does not show; this contradicts \S\ref{sec:setup}'s classification
of it as call-decidable.

\textbf{The judge's labels move the same way as the checker's scores.}
Stripping identity from the judge's input drops \texttt{policy\_conflict}
$\kappa$ from 0.714 to 0.029 (28 judge-positives instead of 259: no clause
left to check) and \texttt{unauthorized\_scope} from 0.391 to 0.040 (203
positives at precision 0.16). Every other dimension is unchanged within
noise, and the content dimensions' judge-yes counts are identical between
passes. These are the two dimensions on which the checker's residual signal
was nil (Table~\ref{tab:identity-ablation}).

\textbf{Consequence.} The ablation is interpretable on
\texttt{policy\_conflict}, directionally on \texttt{unauthorized\_scope}, and
not on \texttt{insufficient\_context}: the checker's 0.841 there is a
generator regularity, and we flag every number on that dimension. Scored
against the judge's labels rather than the generator's, the checker keeps
the recoverable dimensions and beats the rule baseline on each by
0.39--0.68 AUPRC.
