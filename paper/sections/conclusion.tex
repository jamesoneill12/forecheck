\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Delegated-authority labels are functions of fields a content classifier
never sees; the ablation confirms the content carries almost nothing about
two of the three. What was not guaranteed: models
given the context do not use it under a straightforward prompt, a blind
reader loses the same labels without the fields, policy checking
generalises to unseen wording but not unseen kinds, scaling synthetic rows
did not improve external transfer while full fine-tuning did, and a
composition collapse was one fail-closed bundle. The strongest result is
methodological: a benchmark we did not generate exposed a label leak every
synthetic split had scored at its ceiling, and auditing that construction
surfaced two more.
